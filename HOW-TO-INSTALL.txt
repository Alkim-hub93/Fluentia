FLUENTIA: use it like an app on iPhone / iPad

1) Put this whole folder on a free web host (HTTPS is required for installing):
   - Netlify Drop: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this folder onto the page.
   - or GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages: upload the files, keep them in the same folder.
2) On your iPhone or iPad, open the web address in SAFARI.
3) Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
   Fluentia now opens full screen from its own icon, and works offline after the first visit.

Notes
- Your progress is saved on the device (in the browser's storage). Clearing website data erases it.
- Microphone practice (Speaking and spoken role-play replies) works when Fluentia is opened in Safari.
  iOS does not allow speech recognition inside Home Screen apps; everything else works there.
  Turn on Dictation in Settings > General > Keyboard if the microphone is blocked.
- Android: open the address in Chrome, then menu > Install app.
- Desktop: you can also just double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Safari). It needs no server.
