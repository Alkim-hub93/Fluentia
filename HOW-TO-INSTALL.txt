FLUENTIA: permanent install on iPad / iPhone

Free and permanent: GitHub Pages
1) Unzip this folder in the Files app.
2) Create a free account at github.com, then a new PUBLIC repository (for example "fluentia").
3) Tap Add file > Upload files, and upload all 6 files (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the 3 icons). Tap Commit.
4) Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
5) After a minute your address appears, like yourname.github.io/fluentia. Open it in SAFARI.
6) Share > Add to Home Screen. Fluentia opens full screen from its own icon and works offline after the first visit.
   To update later, upload the new index.html to the same repository. The address stays the same.

Alternative: Netlify Drop (drag the folder), then CLAIM the site with a free account within one hour so it is not deleted.

AI tutor
- Needs your own Anthropic API key (console.anthropic.com). Paste it on the AI tutor page or in Settings.
- It works from the Home Screen icon. Type your messages, or tap the microphone key on the iPad keyboard to dictate.
- The key is stored only in this browser on this device. Use a key with a spending limit.

Notes
- Progress is saved per web address, on the device. Do not change the address after you start.
- Microphone recognition inside the app (Speaking page, mic button in role-play) works in Safari, not in Home Screen mode on iOS.
- Desktop: you can also double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
